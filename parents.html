Our promise

The same four rules in every game

  • No ads, no purchases

    Nothing to buy and nothing to tap by accident. Just the game.

  • No tracking

    No accounts, analytics or cookies. We know nothing about your child, and that's the point.

  • No losing

    No timers, scores or game-over screens. A “wrong” tap just gets a silly wiggle.

  • Toddler-proof

    Big targets, no accidental zoom, full screen, and grown-up buttons kept in the corner.

Five-minute setup

Make a game feel like an app

Each game can live on your home screen with its own icon. It opens full screen and keeps working without internet.

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open the game you want.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open the game from its new icon. No Safari bars, the whole screen is the game.

Extra safe: turn on Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access). Triple-click the side button in a game and little fingers can't leave it.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the game you want.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open it from the icon. It runs full screen, with no browser bars.

Extra safe: use App pinning (Settings → Security → App pinning) to keep the screen on the game.

Laptop or computer

  1. Open a game and press the ⛶ button in its corner for full screen.
  2. Every key does something fun, so keyboard bashing is welcome.
  3. Press Esc to leave full screen.

In Chrome or Edge you can also install a game from the address bar's install icon.

Little lessons

What each game practises

Nothing here is a test. But a lot is going on while they play.

GameAgesWhat they practise
Peekaboo1–3Cause and effect, and the idea that things are still there when hidden (object permanence). Perfect first game.
Animal Band1–4Rhythm, high and low sounds, and taking turns with a song. In song mode any tap plays the next note.
Bubble Bath1–4Finger and hand control by rubbing and swiping. A slow, calming game with a happy finish.
Color Splash2–4Colour names (say them together!), making choices, and finishing a picture.
Bedtime2–4Comparing sizes (small, middle, big) and dragging. Ends calm, nice before a nap.
Baby Pop1–4Tapping and swiping, cause and effect. Keyboard bashing counts too.
Find the Animal2–4Listening and first words: animal names in English, Finnish or Bengali.
Feed the Animals2–3Matching (monkey and banana, rabbit and carrot) and food words.
Playing together

Six small tips

  1. Sit with them

    Games for this age work best as a shared moment. Your voice is the best part.

  2. Name what you see

    “A red balloon!” “The big bear!” The games have no words on purpose, so you can add yours.

  3. Keep it short

    A few minutes is plenty. Stop while it's still fun, not when it falls apart.

  4. Let them lead

    Mashing the screen is playing too. There's no wrong way, and no way to lose.

  5. Copy the sounds

    Moo with the cow, hum the lullaby. Babies love it when you join in.

  6. End calm

    Bedtime and Bubble Bath slow things down. Nice ones to finish with.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Are Pienipeli baby games really free?

Yes. All our games are completely free to play online. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or hidden costs.

Do the games have ads?

No. Pienipeli games have no ads at all, so there is nothing for little fingers to tap by accident.

What age are the games for?

Our games are made for babies and toddlers, roughly ages 1 to 4. They need no reading, and every tap does something fun.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. The games run right in your web browser on phones, tablets (including iPad), laptops and computers. There is no app to download and no account to create.

Do you collect any data about my child?

No. There are no accounts, analytics, cookies or trackers. Small settings like sound on or off stay on your own device. See our privacy page.

How can I stop my toddler from leaving the game?

Tap the full-screen button in the game. On iPhone and iPad you can also use Guided Access (Settings, Accessibility, Guided Access) to lock the screen to the game.

Can I put a game on my home screen like an app?

Yes. Open the game, then:

  • iPhone & iPad: tap Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: tap the browser menu ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Each game gets its own icon, opens straight into the game in full screen, and works without internet once it has been opened. See the step-by-step guide above.

Do the games talk or use words?

Only Find the Animal speaks (it asks “Where is the cat?”). The other seven games use no words at all: just music, chimes and playful animal sounds, so they work for every family and every language.

How long should my toddler play?

Short and sweet is best. A few minutes together, then off to something else. The games never push to keep playing: there are no streaks, rewards or “one more level”.
