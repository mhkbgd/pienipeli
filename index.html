FAQ

Questions parents ask

Are Pienipeli baby games really free?

Yes. All our games are completely free to play online. There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions or hidden costs.

Do the games have ads?

No. Pienipeli games have no ads at all, so there is nothing for little fingers to tap by accident.

What age are the games for?

Our games are made for babies and toddlers, roughly ages 1 to 4. They need no reading, and every tap does something fun.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. The games run right in your web browser on phones, tablets (including iPad), laptops and computers. There is no app to download and no account to create.

Do you collect any data about my child?

No. There are no accounts, analytics, cookies or trackers. Small settings like sound on or off stay on your own device. See our privacy page.

How can I stop my toddler from leaving the game?

Tap the full-screen button in the game. On iPhone and iPad you can also use Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access) to lock the screen to the game.

About

About Pienipeli

Pieni peli means "little game" in Finnish, and that's exactly what we make: small, calm, happy games that are a baby's first steps into play.

We believe early screen time, when there is any, should be gentle: soft colours, kind sounds, cause and effect, and nothing that pushes a child to keep playing.
