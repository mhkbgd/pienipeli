Made with parents in mind

Our promise for every Pienipeli game.

  • 🚫

    No ads, no purchases

    Nothing to buy and nothing to tap by accident. Just the game.

  • 🔒

    No tracking

    No accounts, no analytics, no cookies. We don't collect anything about your child.

  • 💛

    No losing

    No timers, scores or game-over screens. Every tap is a little win.

  • 🧸

    Toddler-proof

    Big targets, no accidental zooming, and full-screen mode so little fingers stay in the game.

Tip: Play with your little one. Name the colours together, count the pops, and keep sessions short and sweet.

About Pienipeli

Pieni peli means "little game" in Finnish, and that's exactly what we make: small, calm, happy games that are a baby's first steps into play.

We believe early screen time, when there is any, should be gentle: soft colours, kind sounds, cause and effect, and nothing that pushes a child to keep playing.
