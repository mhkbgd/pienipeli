Privacy

Short and simple, like our games.

In short: we don’t collect any personal information about you or your child.

What we don’t do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No cookies.
  • No third-party services or scripts. Fonts and all game files come from pienipeli.fi itself.

What stays on your device

A few small settings are saved in your browser’s local storage, only so the games remember them next time. They never leave your device and are never sent to us:

  • your language choice,
  • sound and music on or off (and Baby Pop’s speed),
  • which games have already shown their “how to play” hand.

To work offline, the site also keeps a copy of its own game files in your browser. You can remove all of this at any time by clearing the site data in your browser settings.

Spoken words

Only Find the Animal speaks, using the voices built into your device or browser. We don’t send anything anywhere, but some browsers use an online voice service of their own. If you’d rather avoid that, turn the sound off in that game. Our other games don’t speak at all.

Server logs

Like any website, the server that hosts pienipeli.fi may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and secure. We don’t use them to identify anyone.
