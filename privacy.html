Privacy

Short version: we don't collect any personal information about you or your child.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels or advertising.
  • No cookies.
  • No third-party services. Fonts and all game files are served from pienipeli.fi itself.

What stays on your device

Our games may remember simple settings, such as Baby Pop's sound on/off and speed, or the language chosen in Find the Animal, using your browser's local storage. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Spoken words

Find the Animal speaks short phrases like "Where is the cat?" using the voices built into your device or browser. We don't send anything anywhere. Some browsers, though, use an online voice service of their own to speak the text. If you'd rather avoid that, turn the sound off in the game or choose an offline voice in your device settings.

Server logs

Like any website, the server that hosts pienipeli.fi may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and secure. We don't use these to identify anyone.
