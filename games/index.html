Build a House!

Drag the walls, roof, door and windows into place. Then the lights go on and a family moves in.

  • Drag & drop
  • Shapes

Count the Ducks!

Tap the pond and ducks swim in one by one: 1, 2, 3! Every number climbs a musical step.

  • Counting

Tap Tap Race!

Ages 2–4

Pick an animal racer and tap tap tap to the finish flag. Vroom! Everyone gets a gold medal.

  • Tapping
  • Cause & effect

Decorate the Cake!

Drag strawberries, candles, stars and sprinkles onto a big cake. Then light the candles!

  • Creating
  • Drag & drop

Letter Pop!

  • ABC
  • Tapping

Feed the Numbers!

  • Counting
  • Numbers 1–6

Number Train!

Ages 2–4

  • Numbers 1–5
  • Matching

Trace the Letter!

Ages 3–4

Follow the big dotted letter with a finger, leave a rainbow trail and watch it come alive.

  • Letter shapes
  • Pre-writing

Tilt Track!

Ages 3–4

Tilt the phone (or tap left and right) to drive down a winding road and collect musical stars.

  • Steering
  • Hand-eye

Peekaboo!

Ages 1–3

Animals hide behind bushes, curtains and gift boxes. One tap, and peekaboo!

  • Cause & effect
  • Object permanence

Animal Band!

Ages 1–4

Six singing animals make a little piano that always sounds nice. Add a beat, or tap along to a nursery song.

  • Rhythm
  • High & low
  • Music

Bubble Bath!

Ages 1–4

A muddy friend sits in the tub. Rub the mud away in a cloud of bubbles, then watch it shake dry.

  • Finger control
  • Calm play

Color Splash!

  • Choosing
  • Music

Bedtime!
