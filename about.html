Small games, on purpose

Pienipeli is a small collection of browser games for the youngest players, first made for our own children and now shared with families everywhere.

Why we make them

Most apps for little kids are built to keep them tapping: rewards, streaks, ads, “just one more”. We wanted the opposite: games you can hand over without worrying, that are happy to end after five minutes.

So every Pienipeli game is about cause and effect, sound, colour and first discoveries: letters, numbers, sizes and shapes. You tap, something lovely happens. Nobody fails, and nothing nags.

How they’re made

Each game is a small web page. There are no audio files: every sound, animal call and lullaby is made live in the browser. That keeps the games tiny, quick to load, and able to work offline once opened.

Most games use no speech at all, so they work in any home and any language. The website itself can be read in English, Finnish, Bengali and Turkish.

What we’ll never do

  • Show ads, sell anything, or ask for an account.
  • Track your child, or anyone. No analytics, no cookies.
  • Add timers, scores, streaks or “game over”.
  • Make a game harder to put down than it is to pick up.
