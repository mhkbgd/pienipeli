Small games, on purpose

Pienipeli is a tiny collection of browser games for the youngest players. Here's why it exists and how it's made.

Why we make them

Most apps for little kids are built to keep them tapping: rewards, streaks, ads, “just one more”. We wanted the opposite. Games you can hand over without worrying, and that are happy to end after five minutes.

So every Pienipeli game is about cause and effect, sound and colour. You tap, something lovely happens. Nobody fails, and nothing nags.

How they're made

Each game is a single small web page. There are no audio files: every sound, animal call and lullaby is made live in the browser. That keeps the games tiny, quick to load, and able to work offline once opened.

Most of the games use no words at all, so they work in any home and any language. The one that talks, Find the Animal, speaks English, Finnish or Bengali.

What we'll never do

  • Show ads, sell anything, or ask for an account.
  • Track your child, or anyone. No analytics, no cookies.
  • Add timers, scores, streaks or “game over”.
  • Make a game harder to put down than it is to pick up.
